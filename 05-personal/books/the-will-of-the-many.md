# The Will of the Many

**Author:** James Islington
**Series:** The Hierarchy, Book 1 of 3
**Progress:** Completed (2026-04-18)

## Book 1 Summary

Vis Telimus is a seventeen-year-old orphan working in a Catenan prison. His true identity is **Prince Diago of Suus**, a conquered island nation whose royal family was killed when the Catenan Republic invaded. He has never ceded Will, making him physically and mentally stronger than his peers — and, unusually, immune to Sappers.

At the prison he meets **Ulciscor Telimus**, a Quintus of the Military, who notices Vis's immunity. Ulciscor adopts him and sponsors him into the **Catenan Academy** on the island of Solivagus. The price: Vis must spy on **Principalis Veridius**, the Academy's head, whom Ulciscor blames for the death of his brother **Caeror** years earlier. Caeror was investigating the pre-Cataclysm ruins beneath the island.

At the Academy, Vis navigates brutal Class-based promotion challenges and forms a tight-knit group with **Callidus** (his closest friend), **Eidhin** (a Cymrian student he tutors), and **Aequa**. He develops feelings for **Emissa**. In parallel, he's drawn into the **Anguis** rebellion — led by **Estevan/Melior**, his father's former advisor — after surviving their devastating terrorist attack at the Festival of Jovan.

The year culminates in the **Iudicium**, a brutal team competition to retrieve the Heart of Jovan from a sealed tower. During the trial:
* Vis navigates the labyrinth beneath the ruins, guided by instructions that appear on his arm.
* **Emissa betrays him** and stabs him with Will-enhanced force, pushing him from the tower.
* Vis survives, retrieves the Heart, and wins the Iudicium — but **Callidus dies** of his wounds.

Vis wakes to find his **arm amputated** — the "toll" the labyrinth extracted for passage. Veridius reveals Vis's blood is "tainted," claims to be trying to prevent a second Cataclysm, and offers him a place in Religion. Vis refuses and chooses **Governance** under Callidus's father (the Censor) to gather more information on his own terms.

The **Synchronism epilogue** reveals the labyrinth copied Vis across parallel dimensions. One duplicate arrives in a snow-covered rotunda where strangers await "the other from your world." Another meets **Caeror — alive — in Obiteum**, who explains Vis has been duplicated (an old tactic from wars against "the Concurrence") and begins carving "wait" into his arm to save the primary Vis.

## Magic System

### Will

The summation of a person's drive, focus, and physical/mental energy. Tangible: more Will means stronger, sharper, more resilient. Less Will means weaker and duller.

### Ceding

Transferring **half** your Will to another person. Requires touching an **Aurora Columnae** first (pre-Cataclysm artifact) to unlock the ability. Legally required by age eighteen. Structured as a pyramid culminating in the **Princeps** at the top.

**Rank ladder (low → high):** Octavii → Septimus → Sextus → Quintus → Quartus → Tertius → Secundus → Princeps.

Vis never touched an Aurora Columnae, so he can't cede — and nothing can drain him.

### Sappers

Stone devices that forcibly extract **all** of a person's Will, leaving them catatonic. Used on prisoners; the Will funnels upward. Vis is uniquely immune.

### Imbuing

Using excess Will on objects or self:
* **Direct** — strengthen, lighten, illuminate. Self-imbuement turns eyes pitch black (hence dark glasses).
* **Relational** — Will-lock two objects together. Harmonic (move together) or Reactive (attract/repel). Powers Transvects (hovering ships).
* **Conditional** — if-then logic; most complex form.

Natural materials (stone, wood, obsidian) imbue more easily than refined ones (steel). Some Will is always lost in the process (returns when you stop).

## The Hierarchy & Worldbuilding

* **Caten / The Catenan Republic** — Roman-inspired empire built on the Will pyramid. Conquered Suus and many other nations.
* **The Three** — the Republic's factions, edging toward civil war: **Religion**, **Governance**, **Military**. Veridius is tied to Religion; Ulciscor to Military; Callidus's father (the Censor) to Governance.
* **The Catenan Academy** — elite school on the island of **Solivagus**. Sits atop pre-Cataclysm ruins. Students are ranked by Class (Class One = highest). Winning the Iudicium makes you **Domitor**.
* **The Iudicium** — year-end competition where Class Three principals pick helpers and race to retrieve the Heart of Jovan from a sealed tower. Helpers can betray principals and win independently.
* **The Cataclysm** — cataclysmic past event. Veridius believes a second one is imminent.
* **The Ruins / Labyrinth** — beneath Solivagus. Guarded by **Remnants** (undead). The labyrinth is a gateway between realms. Passage requires a physical toll (Vis's arm).
* **Obiteum and Luceum** — two realms accessible through the labyrinth. Caeror is in Obiteum. The inscription warns: *"Sealed against the tools of the enemy after the rending. The passage to Luceum requires a toll to ensure validity."*
* **The Concurrence** — an ancient enemy referenced by Caeror. Tied to the rending, the Cataclysm, and duplication tactics.
* **The Anguis** — rebellion against the Hierarchy, led by Estevan/Melior. Secretly funded in part by elements of the Military.
* **Suus** — Vis's conquered homeland; now the estate of Senator Indol's family.
* **Letens** — the town where Vis grew up working at the prison.
