# Career fact base

*Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD. Pre-filled from <sources>, then corrected in an interview pass with <candidate>.*

This is the only source for facts on a tailored resume. The rules for choosing and writing them are in the `building-exec-resumes` skill.

**Status tags.**
- `sourced: <where>` means a record backs it. LinkedIn, the candidate's site, and "<candidate>, <date>" are all self-reported and don't verify each other. Only HR records, company publications, and filings count as independent.
- `estimate` means the candidate's own figure; the resume words it as approximate.
- `needs-check: <what>` means it can't go on a resume until the candidate confirms it.

**Attribution:** each achievement uses the strongest verb the candidate used. A resume may never use a stronger one.

**Story:** every achievement records whether the candidate has an interview story for it: `yes`, a public write-up, or `to confirm at review`. A bullet drafted from a `to confirm` entry gets cut at review unless the candidate supplies the story.

**Themes:** `builder`, `scaler`, `gm-commercial`, `org-building`, `platform-depth`, `ai-hands-on`, `m-and-a-integration`. Add or rename themes to fit the candidate.

**Sources used:** list every source here, with dates. Where sources disagree, the candidate picks the canonical version, and each mismatch goes into `public-profile-fixes.md`.
